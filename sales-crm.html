PROJECT 03

AI Sales CRM Assistant

An AI-powered CRM workflow that captures new leads, evaluates their potential, prioritizes opportunities, and stores lead information in a database.

View GitHub →

The Problem

Sales teams can receive many leads through different channels. Manually reviewing, qualifying, prioritizing, and updating every lead can become repetitive and make it harder to focus on the most valuable opportunities.

The Solution

I built an AI-powered Sales CRM Assistant that combines lead capture, database storage, AI qualification, and prioritization into one workflow.

The system uses AI to evaluate incoming leads and helps identify high-priority opportunities so that sales teams can focus their attention where it matters most.

How It Works

01 — Capture

A new lead enters the system through a form.

02 — Store

Lead information is stored in Supabase.

03 — Qualify

Gemini AI evaluates the lead and determines its potential.

04 — Prioritize

The system identifies high-priority leads for follow-up.

Key Features

Lead capture form
Supabase database storage
AI lead qualification
Lead scoring
Priority identification
CRM data management

Technology Stack

Python Gemini AI Streamlit Supabase PostgreSQL REST APIs GitHub
